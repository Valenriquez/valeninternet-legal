Content Planner

Content Planner is a personal tool built and used by a single creator (valeninternet) to plan short-form videos on a calendar and publish them to the creator's own TikTok and YouTube accounts at a scheduled time (Mexico City time).

What it does

  • Lets the owner upload their own videos and write captions and hashtags.
  • Schedules each video for a date and time.
  • At that time, uploads the video to the owner's own connected TikTok and YouTube accounts.

The app is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
